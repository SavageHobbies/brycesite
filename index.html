Proprietary Analytical Frameworks

Sink Ratio (η)

Sink Efficiency Ratio

Measures the ratio of non-extractive token burn sinks to total inflationary issuance, proving economic longevity before capital is deployed.

5 Pillars

Five-Pillar Evaluator

A 21-point auditing standard inspecting team vestment, contract permissions, liquidity locks, sink mechanics, and player retention.

YpH

Yield-per-Hour Metric

Calculates net realized hourly return accounting for gas overhead, asset depreciation, and skill barrier adjustments.
